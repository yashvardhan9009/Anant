Earth explorer sources

- Global satellite: Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX IT Services GmbH,
  containing modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016, CC BY 4.0.
  https://s2maps.eu / https://maps.eox.at / https://eox.at
- US aerial imagery: USGS National Map NAIP Plus, USDA NAIP and contributing
  public-domain orthophotography. https://imagery.nationalmap.gov
- Elevation: Mapzen terrain tiles, USGS, SRTM and other contributors.
  Full underlying dataset notices: https://www.mapzen.com/rights/
- Orbital fallback map: Solar System Scope / INOVE, based on NASA imagery,
  CC BY 4.0. https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
- City index: GeoNames cities15000, retrieved 2026-10-26, CC BY 4.0.
  https://www.geonames.org / https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/
- Optional Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles: Google Maps Platform. Requires the
  operator's restricted browser key and applicable Google Maps terms.
  Google and dataset credits are displayed by Cesium at runtime.

Attribution remains visible in cinema mode. Satellite/aerial imagery is not
live, and resolution and acquisition dates vary by location. Public imagery
is draped over terrain and does not reconstruct individual buildings in 3D.
